# G-Stand

> **"An Intelligence Beside Yours"** — A verifiable behavioral core and wearable hardware architecture designed to govern physical AI interactions without overstepping human boundaries.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
As Edge AI and Physical AI expand into real-world environments (agriculture, industrial operations, and daily workflows), existing systems fall into two flawed paradigms: 
1. **Total Automation:** High capital friction, high failure costs, and complete replacement of human agency.
2. **Ungoverned Chat Interfaces:** Disconnected software agents that lack physical contextual boundaries.

**G-Stand** introduces an **interaction governance layer** and wearable hardware architecture. Rather than replacing human labor, G-Stand acts as a verified behavioral core that integrates safely into high-friction environments, ensuring AI stands *beside* humans rather than overstepping them.

---

## 2. Core Architecture
The system architecture is structured around strict behavioral constraints and real-time sensor integration:

- **Behavioral Core (v0 "SEE & SPEAK" Baseline):** Frozen decision-making and interaction logic ensuring transparent, predictable, and boundary-respecting responses.
- **Wearable Interaction Layer:** Bridges raw data streams from edge devices to human-side operational execution.
- **CONSEI Integration:** Built on organizational principles examining the intersection of biological systems and artificial intelligence coordination.

---

## 3. Visual Domain Testing & Demos
G-Stand’s behavioral core has been tested across multiple physical and visual domains:
- **Agricultural Monitoring:** Edge-based boundary and resource oversight.
- **Professional Workflows:** Real-time human-AI collaboration without agency override.
- **Daily Operational UI:** Low-latency feedback loops designed for fast physical execution.
